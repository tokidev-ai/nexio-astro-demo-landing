Rules

  • Never two gold buttons in the same viewport.
  • Hover displacement is 1px. Anything larger reads as the page moving, not the button.
  • Only transform, opacity, color and box-shadow animate — never width, height, or margin.
  • Icons are Lucide SVG at 16px with a 2px stroke, and always aria-hidden when the label already says it.
