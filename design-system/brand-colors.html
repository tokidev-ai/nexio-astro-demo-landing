Legibility check

The three text roles, shown on the two surfaces they are actually allowed to appear on.

Bone on ink — body copy.

Muted on ink — supporting copy.

Faint on ink — meta, 12px, never body.

Bone on stone — card copy.

Muted on stone — card meta.

Faint on stone — timestamps.

White on gold — CTA label.

The only approved use of the gold fill.

Anti-patterns

Explicitly out of bounds for this brand: neon accents, purple/pink AI gradients, pure #000 grounds, and gold used as a text color on dark (it fails at body size).

Gold as body text on ink — 4.0:1, fails normal text.

Below the 4.5:1 threshold for body copy, though it clears 3:1 for large text. Use bone or muted for sentences; keep --gold for fills and rules. When gold text is genuinely wanted, use --gold-hi (6.7:1).
