Motion

Count-up runs once when the strip enters the viewport — 1.2s, power2.out, never re-triggering on scroll-back.

  • The final value is always present in the DOM. The animation counts to it, so a crawler, a screen reader, and a reduced-motion visitor all get the real number.
  • Under prefers-reduced-motion the tween is skipped entirely and the end state renders immediately — not a faster count.
  • font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums is mandatory, otherwise the strip visibly reflows on every frame.
  • The whole strip is one ScrollTrigger, not four.
