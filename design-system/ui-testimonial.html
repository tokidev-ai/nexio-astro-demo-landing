Rules

  • Quote caps at roughly 14 words. If it needs more, it is a case study, not a testimonial.
  • Face, name, and role are required. Initials in a ring stand in until a real portrait exists — never a generic avatar illustration.
  • Three-up on desktop, stacked below 768px. No carousel: three cards fit, so auto-rotation would add pause controls, live-region announcements, and focus handling for no gain.
  • Cards reveal with a 0.08s stagger on scroll; content is fully present without JS.
