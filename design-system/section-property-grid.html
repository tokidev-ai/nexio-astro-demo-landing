Behaviour

  • 3-up ≥1024px, 2-up ≥768px, 1-up below. Card min-width 280px so nothing ever crushes.
  • Entrance stagger is 0.06s with grid: 'auto', capped at 8 items — beyond that the last cards feel laggy.
  • Filtering re-runs the stagger on the surviving cards only. Removed cards fade, they do not collapse-animate, which would thrash layout.
  • The count lives in role="status", so the change is announced without stealing focus.
